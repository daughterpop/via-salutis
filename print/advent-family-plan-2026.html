An Advent Plan for the Household (2026)

The Church in the United States sets no required fast in Advent. Every Friday is still a day of penance: no meat for those 14 and older, or, outside Lent, another act of penance approved by the U.S. bishops. The Ember days are optional, an old and voluntary practice. Children do not fast. Their part is a small sacrifice, chosen with a parent and ended gladly at Christmas. A prayer for the table

Said at supper after lighting the wreath, through all four weeks. This is the old collect of the First Sunday of Advent.

Stir up thy power, we beseech thee, O Lord, and come: that from the threatening dangers of our sins we may be rescued by thy protection, and saved by thy deliverance. Who livest and reignest with God the Father, in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, world without end. Amen.

All answer: Come, Lord Jesus. (Revelation 22:20)

The O Antiphons, December 17 – 23

One each evening at supper, the week before Christmas. Each ends with the same word: come.

  1. Dec 17 · O Wisdom, who came forth from the mouth of the Most High, reaching from end to end and ordering all things mightily and sweetly: come, and teach us the way of prudence.
  2. Dec 18 · O Adonai, and Leader of the house of Israel, who appeared to Moses in the fire of the burning bush and gave him the law on Sinai: come, and redeem us with an outstretched arm.
  3. Dec 19 · O Root of Jesse, who stands as a sign for the peoples, before whom kings shall keep silence and to whom the nations shall pray: come, and deliver us, and tarry not.
  4. Dec 20 · O Key of David, and Scepter of the house of Israel, who opens and no one shuts, who shuts and no one opens: come, and lead the captive from prison, who sits in darkness and the shadow of death.
  5. Dec 21 · O Dayspring, splendor of light eternal and sun of justice: come, and enlighten those who sit in darkness and the shadow of death.
  6. Dec 22 · O King of the nations, and their desire, the cornerstone who makes both one: come, and save the race of man, whom you formed from the dust of the earth.
  7. Dec 23 · O Emmanuel, our King and Lawgiver, the expectation of the nations and their Savior: come, and save us, O Lord our God.

Who is bound

Under 14No fast, no required abstinence. Small sacrifices only. Never take a meal from a child.
14 and olderFriday abstinence, or in the U.S. outside Lent, another penance.
18 to 59The same. Fasting in Advent is voluntary.
60+, sick, pregnant, nursingNot bound to fast. Pray and give alms.

A word of care

A small penance kept to December 24 is better than a large one dropped by the 6th. If fasting has become a way to punish the body, or food is already a struggle, set the fast aside and talk to a priest and a doctor. This is a rule of prayer, not medical advice.
